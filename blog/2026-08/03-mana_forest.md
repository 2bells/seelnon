# Gold and Geo

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/1.jpg)

To replenish mana and refill some potions I went to explore a bit... some fun 'broken poetry' should be cute to go along as well.
 

---

Through the sea of gold I swam into the worlds unseen

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/2.jpg)

My way was blessed by yellow and the sun, shimmering with wind

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/3.jpg)

In the lands 'Unseen' I saw a bee, 
Working, full of might

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/4.jpg)

And as she worked, the lands around me
Transformed, coloring my sight

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/5.jpg)

And then I noticed on the meadows
A forest line beckoning me in

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/6.jpg)

I stepped in further into flowers, berries, fruits and height

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/7.jpg)

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/8.jpg)

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/9.jpg)

Trees swayed in silent cover, playfully, to my delight

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/10.jpg)

More berries with sunlight followed

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/11.jpg)

And then I met a family of ghosts

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/12.jpg)

That took me to their Lord of Geo

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/13.jpg)

I talked to Lord and as my blessings
I met a fearsome fella at the end

![img](blog/2026-08/img/forest/14.jpg)

He ignored me... that's the end.

```
thought of a day

silence of the woods
speaks in many words
```
