There is a fun comet traveling around our solar system as of right now.
Lore has it: the glow of reached the size bigger than Jupiter.

Idea behind it is that it releases gases, but those get ionised in the vacuum of space and it becomes plasma, creating the glow.
Seems like heat can make things glow in a big and beautiful way. Poetic even. Especially that comet's core is 1-2km in size, while the glow is over 250 000 km
