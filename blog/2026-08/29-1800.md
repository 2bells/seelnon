## Learning

I guess I found my natural weakness of pure calculation limit [Too lazy to draw lines]... there is something I wanted to test though, when it comes to learning. I noticed it with Genshin Guitar Hero: 'going beyond caring'. Meaning you just spend all your energy, focus, attention, drive and motivation for the day, but you keep going through motions anyway. It seem to activate some secondary networks in the brain when it cannot rely on 'trained responses', so you have to 'reinvent' how to focus and bring yourself back to 'not missing every move' or 'not missing every note'...

I tried it with chess a bit, but next morning it became worse somehow)))
First time I was like: yeah it is doable and then it went all the way down of me missing every set play. And the main problem: everything becomes SO uncomfortable. It is like bot sets your pieces up for failure. Every idea I would have suddenly there is a pawn in the way. Then I would find the move, calculate like ~4 ahead, do the move... bro just moves the piece away in the back line.
I started to think to give up on playing bots, because if at 1300 they already not doing much 'human' it might be loosing the point. But somehow 1400 are fine)))
