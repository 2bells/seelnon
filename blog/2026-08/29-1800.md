# Elo 1800

Omg! Who is this guy?!

![img](blog/2026-08/img/1800.jpg)

Tbh, I struggle against some of the 1300 bot setups who calculate 4 moves ahead, but make a lot of positional mistakes that could be punished easily.
They are pretty much my cryptonite. This game on top was against raw 1400 elo on chess.com

I do get a lot of 'great' moves and a lot of times I can even see them, but when it comes to staking threats and moving 'under cover' it is sooo bad.
One game I even cheated some moves by looking up suggestions, I didn't play some of them when they have been clearly not a move I ever make, like randomly a4 after some queen movement of the enemy... And the game was just a clear win out of nowhere as well. And it even looked really interesting, because it was a combination of me doing my stuff and sometimes listening to the suggestion if move was something I would able to see the reason why... it felt very easy. Next game I play by myself and I'm just getting outplayed on all fronts)))

tbh, the idea to look at suggestions, because I was studying oppening variations. Sometimes I would get -1 after the opening, because me = bad. Usually when there are a lot of bishops and queens involved early on. I was able to get myself more comfortable with knight pins because of that (for some reason I didn't consider that if I'm threatened by a pawn, I can just take the knight).

But these 1300 guys are just removing me from existance:

![img](blog/2026-08/img/1300_lock.jpg)

But when I go up to 1350-1400, it is back to comfy mode.

I guess I found my natural weakness of pure calculation limit... there is something I wanted to test though, when it comes to learning. I noticed it with Genshin Guitar Hero: 'going beyond caring'. Meaning you just spend all your energy, focus, attention, drive and motivation for the day, but you keep going through motions. It seem to activate some secondary networks in the brain when it cannot rely on 'trained responses', so you have to 'reinvent' how to focus and bring yourself back to 'not missing every move' or 'not missing every note'...

I tried it with chess a bit, but next morning it became worse somehow)))
First time I was like: yeah it is doable and then it went all the way down of me missing every set play. And the main problem: everything becomes SO uncomfortable. It is like bot sets your pieces up for failure. Every idea I would have suddenly there is a pawn in the way. Then I would find the move, calculate like ~4 ahead, do the move... bro just moves the piece away in the back line.
I started to think to give up on playing bots, because if at 1300 they already not doing much 'human' it might be loosing the point. But somehow 1400 are fine)))

Whatever, I like playing bots, don't want to entangle myself into the system, yet. Or maybe ever. I feel like I can land myself around 1100 with + - 200 depending how locked-in I am. But the good thing I found big weakness that I have a specific bot type to work around and see how it goes.
