# Like Impact

![img](blog/2026-08/img/like_impact.jpg)

Peaceful exploration of Genshin Impact be like...

![img](blog/2026-08/img/like_impact2.jpg)

It is such a great idea to attach a 'value' to a very intrusive mechanic.
It has to be a common knowledge that people actually kill each other unironically for 'internet points' nowadays. And I do get the appeal: 'Death Stranding' had it, let's make it as well! 
But this is the first day of the patch and it is already that bad... And if we say that: ok we will refine it later and it will be 'friends only' or only from 'hot moms in your area' so there will be a reason to 'Like' those constructs, but... while going to this 'patch of signs' from a nearby teleport I was FORCED into 5! cutscenes back to back: 
- approached the statue -> cutscene! 
- enter nearby cave -> cutscene! 
- run away from that cave like fire and climbed the mountain -> cutscene!
- walked a trail, enemies spawn -> cutscene!
- found interesting place with a door -> cutscene!
- tried to climb the place -> L+Ratio hardcoded max cold, die!

![img](blog/2026-08/img/explora.jpg)

What happened? Now it is impossible to just 'explore' and enjoy the wilderness with level design accompanied by the music and naturally 'stumble into fun'. 
It starts to resemble the 'western downfall' of games, when suddenly everything needs to be curated. 'Fun' is what developers make, not what you enjoy. Meaning Genshin as of now on a massive turning point turning into complete slop or rebouncing with understanding that 'this is not the way forward'.

Even though I'm massive Genshin apologist, this is just too much... it feels like a heavy leakage happened of 'yellow paint philosophy' from people who never play games, but 'learn how to make them' instead. It always happens to all industries that become popular enough: bissnuss manns spot the spot and start actual invasion of space, because they want to attach themselves to something BIG and IMPORTANT. Self fulfilling prophecy, but... can we turn the wheel please?

Let's see how they develop it, I did like the side dash during aiming, that is a peak button to press.
