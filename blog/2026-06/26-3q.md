## Card Checker

![cards](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/cu9i4afuoseyey87vufsn/CN_stuff3.jpg?rlkey=5h8usrpyupfo3lur8c1z8gql4&st=qublezce&raw=1)

Cards to check yourself are also here. Interactive and just 'flip a card' setup if you don't want to enter anything... But you can do:
- 'pinyin check', 
- 'translation check', 
- 'character check'. 
Characters are pick from 12. Pinyin has grepping [searches all pinyin available and asks you what you mean, so you will get multiple 'yi' or 'fang' responces as well]

## Categories

![cats](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/n1ubgkc61kem4v5mm9xo5/CN_stuff4.jpg?rlkey=liuis1kwd9piye4qzdt5j8bh6&st=3787rmiv&raw=1)

Also you can just do your own setup. AND! the whole thing is actually just a .json injection. It uses .js, but it pretty much sends you 1 giant variable into the 'main constructor' that grabs the data and data injects into the whole ecosystem.
So in theory you can grab any subject, give an example of .js to any AI and make your own in seconds as a 'study source'. Even if not adding anything it is possible to just edit the array and instead of 'ancient.js' being about some spiritual stuff it becomes about anything you want.... idk... cats... or food.
