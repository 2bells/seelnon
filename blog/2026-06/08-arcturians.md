# Concept Bruting

![img](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/2slkq4so8v2zj07d26kfm/CONCEPT_BRUTE_1780855813547.jpg?rlkey=3f4d6mdwpzbtuubnhjsmpkxuv&st=c6kua2x3&raw=1)

Somehow I ended up drawing a blue lady. Was doing brush tests with new settings and she just 'happened'. And while she was appearing I started to think about 'Arcturian' race [Blue people from UFO lore]. And it naturally lead me to the 'Avatar' and how James Cameron was talking about that he REALLY wanted to make Avatar for over 30 years (even 50 in some cases)... And watching a supposed Magnum Opus of the guy, it felt like a common fairy tale with a sci-fi coat of paint. What so special about the wait and a genuine 'need' to show it?

But then, there are similar stories, but in the UFO lore: like Corey Goode (also blue, but Avians) and Tony Rodriguez of '20 and Back' with the whole Secret Space Program and Dark Fleet lore. It goes something like this: greys abducting you, pushing you into a 'recreated body' and you just doing 20 years of service as a 'space slave'. As lore it is very fun and those people from '20 and Back' do have genuine trauma / memories from all of that... So even if you are like: "Haha! There is always a BOOK! If you are a writer, just say so!". There are some interesting points and body language showcasing reality of the situation, at least for them.

So I started to think: what if James Cameron is a '20 and Back' guy. Because there is no way to go about it and trying to prove that 'hey guys, I worked on Ceres for 8 years and we are fighting against 'bugs', so Starship Trooper is a documentary'... it's not a good look for your social gameplay loop. So main idea is to create a fiction with your experience as a narrative to put it out there and not claim any 'reality' to it. Corey Goode later also claimed it all as fiction, even though there are 'talks', not even 'podcasts' recorded with a guy... and... let's be honest, it doesn't sound like someone parading their 'fiction' around.

## Issues

Some problems of '20 and back' program are: it could be just a simulation an ayy lmao puts you in for shits and giggles. But, some people been saying: "yeah, I did 20 and back and I was just a girl on the reception for all these years on Aries Prime on Mars", which is the most weird thing, as it is 'too normal' to the point that it rings true.

Other could be: an adjacent reality that's in a deep 40k situation. So you are thrown into a place no one wants to be in. (multiverse theories are ok right now in quantum physics, so... not too schizo).

And of course, just screen memories and injections by... something for some reason. But to have a full 20 years of experience is interesting. + '20 and back' is too weird and niche to be a wagon to jump on.

BUT! James Cameron... and his movies: Terminator, Abyss, Aliens, Avatar... not saying anything, but that's interesting. Funny enough that some UFO lore does report 'spaceships' being like a submarine. And funny coincidence "I waited for technology to catch up"... when he was talking about Avatar.

So 'Avatar' has that feel of:
- James, it is just plants?... And water... I mean... it looks good, yeah.
- Yeah, that's the point.
- So that's what you wanted to show so badly... 30-50 years, James.
- [redacted]` Yeah, because I've been there. `[/redacted]
- I wanted to push VFX to its limits to showcase a truly alien world!
- Understandable, have a nice day.

Mostly it is an idea: 'where does imagination of oneself comes from'? I drew a lady and almost was asking her: 'how did you happen?'. Because she started to give of a 'vibe', like I'm painting a genuine portrait, not just me testing some brushes. I mean, I don't mind doing a commission for a fellow Arcturian, just hit me up if you need another one.

## But There is More

If ayy lmaos are similar to what we have in our 'textbooks', they all are humanoids. Why?

There are 2 things science people actually like to contradict themselves with:
- 1st: 'if it is alien, it has to be very weird and alien'.
- 2nd: 'naturally there should be a dominating species that wipe out competition' (Gause's Law).

So if we manage timelines and assume that we are in the 'deep history' of the universe and not 'OMG we just spawned'. It means universe is fully under control of a specific race type that is humanoid, so they do humanoid things. We, humans, could make anime characters into anything, yet they are more like an architectural hybrid of cats and people. Compare their foreheads and eye/nose/mouth placements to a cat, here you go. 

Also humanoid robots are very stupid to make, but we still do them anyway. Almost every character in any game you can play as is a Humanoid, while enemies are a big spider lady, bugs and old people.

And the most interesting one: a very important gameplay loop of a race: compatible DNA for self improvement. And that needs 'breeding grounds' / farms...

Fermi Paradox... is because your are not invited to the 'party'.

Btw, if humans can make anime ladies reality, for sure for sure number 1 priority will be DNA compatibility.

But if to drop the act a bit: DNA is cute, but it is the 'traditional sci-fi' thinking. Most likely there is a need for a specific consciousness recipes / soul types. That is impossible to engineer and a specific 'recipe' has to go through its life as one and only. No guardrails. Now this is a true delicacy.

Even then, let's say main gameplay loop: spawn -> live -> make mistakes -> go to hell to wash and learn -> repeat. Advanced race could be like: nah... hell is not fun, let's outsource it! So you spawn -> go to school -> fed a human soul full of mistakes -> you learn -> don't need to go to hell -> profit. Your Arcturian children are educated and don't need to live 20 lifetimes to learn how to talk to a girl.
