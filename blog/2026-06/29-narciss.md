## What is Narcissism?

It is not an act of looking in the mirror and reminding yourself of how beautiful you are, even though the naming came from that story [2]. In reality, it is a defensive mechanism that is built to protect oneself and own ego by creating layers and layers of logical barriers [3].

It could be developed by parenting of constantly praising a kid. Or when a kid was able to successfully parry attacks on their own ego by utilizing a logical defense. Occurs, when there were no pushback from the environment, so the mechanism is assimilated into a pattern and used as a reliable strategy. And a lot of times used very successfully until much later in life, when 'facade' starts to show cracks and people/work/results start to distance themselves, causing a person to actually be confused about inner workings of the world, rather then suspect own 'miss management', because development of that type of defences wasn't a conscious activity in the first place [4].

Issue: Mechanism protects own ego, so there is 0 work has been done with it, making it pretty much impossible to 'treat', because in order to start working on it: one has to assume the problem is 'them'. But during an assumption they themselves become an attacker on their own ego, so it triggers a loop of mental gymnastics: "it's not me, it is you". And, because the mainstream explanation of narcissism is about a guy looking at own reflection for far too long: it has the perfect loophole: "I'm not doing that, so I'm not it. I'm misunderstood by definition". And the smarter the person the more loopholes they can conjure to protect themselves only strengthening defensive layers.

## Why bad, if works?

Why is it a defect, rather than a character trait: those people are very annoying to deal with and by pushing right buttons they may become dangerous. And the most damage is usually done to people around them, because naturally a narcissistic person would pick a friend/ship they have easy time gaslighting, creating prey/predator scenario instead of healthy relationships built on trust, openness and meaningful mutual fulfillment. Their career choices also will lead to fields of concrete data, so they can easily state 'objective reality' without a need to question 'truths'.

Problems usually occur after relatively long contact, because a few conflicts had to happen in the first place. During the conflict a narcissistic person would go into a few specific things:
- only their playing field [only my way, or no way]
- categorising [you: student, me: physicist]
- restricting input [we are not talking about that at the moment]
- reminder of own capabilities [phd, experience, work years, achievements]
- reminder of incapabilities [you are a junior, no projects, l+ratio]
- reminder of sacrifices [I did so much for you]
- pressure [any ultimatum of sorts]
- reminder of past interactions [remember when you've been drowning?]
- over explaining of obvious things [it is =2, because math is science]
- staying on the topic they were right about for 2 hours
- pushing under the rug a topic they were a bit off about
- over exhaustion to make another person to give up the conversation
- praying on memory [you don't remember, but I do. Very clearly]
- overall gambits and injections

Even though it is a list, it is simple: Do everything to grab the wheel, turn it into a comfy ride for themselves, make sure to remind why they are in the driver seat.

Why is it annoying? Their comfort becomes number 1 priority, everyone else's is negotiable.

Worst comes later. This type of a ride is not sustainable and genuine human slip ups happen all the time. Usually: talks big, does nothing. For a well adjusted person it is not a problem: they can say sorry, they can try again, they can tune down talk, cry about it and regroup. But they cannot 'erase' the situation from existence. 

For a narcissistic person it becomes a 'death sentence', because now this is 100% direct attack on their ego that never was hit before. So even the small thing will be 100% Crit Rate, x10 Crit Damage.

And this is bad... because all these years they were able to wiggle themselves out of everything and now they got got! Everyone wants a piece. [There was a situation like that with a streamer. Has a Dr.K episode as well and can lead into a good case study.] 
What happens: those people genuinely do not understand why it is happening. So, instead of reflecting on the problem and accepting the reaction as genuine, they activate 2nd layer of defences: double down and say that reaction of others is out of proportion and force everyone back onto 'their playing filed', stating: 'hey, you are making a big deal out of it for no reason and your reaction is inappropriate in the first place'. 'If you keep doing it, you are gone'. So 2nd layer of defences is when it can become very dicy, because logically: no human = no problem.

The more this type of situations stockpile the more cracks open up and options are simple:

## How to fight

Emotionless non-engagement with self centered statements.

Don't play the game, don't go on the field. There is no 'home field' no 'away field' if game is not played. All you need to do is to state your own position in reference to yourself without emotional coat.

Example:
- I understand your point, but these conversations only bring negativity and do not help.
- Always reminding about your position doesn't solve the situation.
- I do not have trust in your words as I, myself, do not see any tangible results.
- I don't want anything, our conversations are not productive. I would rather not have them at all.
- I appreciate your concern, help and everything you do, but I genuinely need to distance myself from you and those conversations. Our interactions lack meaning and things I'm searching for.

Of course it will follow with barrage of 'gotcha' hooks to get into emotional territory. But main idea is: they rely on defences that derive from interaction and proper positioning of the pieces [10]. All what is possible is to not give them any ammo and state only from 'your position' and use only logical hooks from your PoV of your ego. 'This is not helping'. 'I don't have trust'. And standing your ground.

Because those type of conversations will go something like this:
- So you are the x/y/z now, huh!
- How will you do z/y/x then?
- How dare you to speak like that!
- After everything I did...
- I can't believe you became like that!
- I should've x/y/z
- You not worth my time...

And it will go on, because their gameplan is to get you emotional and get you into their normal comfy loop. You will have options to irritate them more or better option is to keep agreeing and stating that 'it is not helping' and repeating your position. (ex. 'I'm searching for meaning and you are not providing it').

It can go bad to the point of physical abuse, throwing your clothes away, breaking your things and insisting that you should get out... and you kind of will be saving yourself time, health and nerves by doing so.
