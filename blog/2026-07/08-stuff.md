## Lore

Also doing some merging of my lore ideas from way back from 2012-2014 to recent ones of 2024 and moving them under 1 bigger universe. I had a detective story of MC being able to see 'feelings' as 'autumn world' so every feeling represented as different flowers. Other was about Stella and 2 Earth, that 1 lives heaven, other hell and that Stella puts people to sleep and syphons energies and distributes them into different 'Earths'. So she is like an AI system that appears in your dreams to repurposed your soul. The Melmet: massive one about a development of a 'helmet' that messes with your consciousness. It goes to the point of creating worlds that are built inside of peoples minds and the whole spectrum of problems after that. 'Red Eyes' about living 'time' inside dolls that are built to take entities from the limbo, souls that got stuck in-between worlds. Ex-Mansion with the whole fluid reality that you can change into an 'elemental demon' from basic human, because you got under the specific weather and didn't wear a coat. 'Deathless Traveler' with robots acting from un-reality, invisible to humans, creating hive-mind slavery, while humans still think they are acting out of a free will, while they are under their Big Brothers for centuries already [with a fun parallel that building AI is an instruction from the machines, because that way they can learn, because they lack natural evolutionary cycles]. 'Henrietta's world' with the whole fallen angels and dreamers who lock themselves up in a dream and just everyone being sad and lonely, because their Goddess has seized to function. Etc.

So watching Warhammer 40k lore to see how it is done and main 'hooks' that transition you from a lowly Heretic to a pure devotee of the God-Emperor.
