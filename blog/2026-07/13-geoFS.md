Long story long: got news about Google Earth adding 'flight simulator', which is more or less just a vibe coded camera controls to do 'fly by's -> got interested if any API could be yonked... no success, but then found about CesiumJS -> tried my hand at it -> found some people already built flight simulator with that -> cloned the repo, it asks for API keys to some servers that need your coat, glasses and your bike upfront... -> sad -> started to think about what can I do about this -> found out that Cesium itself has user made content and a lot of tutorials on how to make your own 'fly' things -> found a blog post that talks about Geo FS...

## Geo FS

It it peak! Pretty much MSFS, but in the browser. Doesn't explode your graphics card, doesn't need 20 billion terrabates of spaces on your physical RAM, doesn't ask for anything, not even plug and play, just 'play'.

[Just Play](https://www.geo-fs.com/)

Is this better than MSFS? no... it has barely any functionality of a cockpit, lacks a lot of polish, sound design - never heard of her, some physics are... not 100%, but 90% there. But it seems like you can hotwire things just from F12 menu as well and that's interesting to say the least.

But overall... it is peak!
