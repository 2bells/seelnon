Interesting part about it: the main idea of this thing is 'feature crept to extreme'. Maybe my dis-interest in it because it is so BIG, that even go to feature complete is LOOOONG process.

It has overworld map that probably will have additional overworld map on top of it [with Gods and cosmic horrors boss battles, kek]. It uses Einstein equasions of spacetime distortion / relativity / space tension combined with some random sauce to make look physics-y, all wrapped around some cosmic ideologies with Zodiacs that are mirroring 'Lusts' for specific things, such as 'Lust for Symmetry' and that affects the resources you get. The whole thing is to 'swim around' and remove 'folds' of spacetime and when you remove them it leaves holes that now you have to patch up and then it transforms into normal type RTS with commanding units and clearing 'maps' that will be like Path of Exile maps.

And instead of basebuilding it is inner factory inside of the mothership that works like Factorio with endless space with ability to later have '12 brains' that will be featuring specific upgrades that are referencing Zodiacs. (why Zodiacs? I asked AI about some things and their count was 12... and it kind of gave me Zodiacs, beause those are 12... and it was fitting... so here we are). So you pre-build your units in the factory to use for RTS maps.

And later all points of interest on the spacetime map will be full solar systems with terraforming and colony simulator style mixed with stardew valley.

It is as stupid as it sounds, but... idk... it makes sense in my head for some reason. And I want to introduce Walter Russell's frequency based periodic table as well, this thing:

![img](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GOa5lLwbQAAbn99.jpg)

But make it like every solar system has their own 'Vibium' and 'Monogen' as elements and just go wild on that. With player just planting stuff and then creating systems to produce a specific frequency wave to create an element and just stitch together weird spaceships and upgrade the mothership with different colors/elements... And use one of my older ideas of utilising color data as 'stats' so hue/lightness/saturation are giving different stats and you may hunt the galaxy to find 'perfect red' element to give you MAX ATK.

Overall it suppose to be very cozy type experience: you just swim about, do stuff. Everything should be omega procedural, but in 1 place + - [the factory place]... so like No Man's Sky, but you collect 'bodyparts' and create things with them, rather than landing on a planet and see the combination. Of coruse it is 2D, but I want to do 'shapez' style factory, that you get an element, melt it into a shape, split the shape, add it to a 'unit' and now you have OP flying tank blueprint of own design. [Ork style from Warhammer 40k]
