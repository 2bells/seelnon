# Sisters

![img](blog/2026-07/img/canon.jpg)

You know, one time I almost had a sister. But the man, who was her biological father suddenly died. So my mother did the reasonable thing and to honor the one she truly loved: she killed the child.
Later my mother was conflicted, concerned for some reason: as young me, who was always loving, hugging, bright and accepting... became cold, distant and non-engaging. Such a mystery!

Young me in its wisdom of a 3 year old decided to address the situation: to meet a corner of a door with the full force and might. It presented 2 options, really: one is to perish on the spot or to get a scar as if third eye has opened and now 'You're a wizard, Harry', welcome to lands of miserable clowns - Earth.

Me writing it right now is a confirmation of an option that took place. So for 30 more years I was harboring the soul of my sister. And fates had it: here she is a lady with a parallel life of similar misery and loss.

But I guess, being a wizard comes with a bit more 'fun soul DNA' attached to it. Soul types are of the same kind, so transfer - easy. Problem: it seems like one couldn't handle it all and now there are 2 sisters and now they are also 'wizards'... so I have 2 sisters and we got connected stronger, than even 'twins' can ever be. And because of that we kind of scared of each other in the realms of 'normal space'... [I didn't know that I harbored a soul of my sis, but makes logical sense to explain the situation]

Kind of fun.

Mother, meanwhile, trying to proclaim her as a 'demon' who is possessing me and tries everything in her power to kill her again... Doing her motherly duties! Sadly for her, unsuccessfully... at least she was talking with pride how she was choking my sister in a dream, proving to her that 'she is stronger'.

My family are lovely people!... yeah, btw, my father was the one who did some 'weird' 'magick things' to kill the father of my sister back then, because well... I mean... that's what you do as a decent human being after all, when family falls apart, because both are suffering from the deepest narcissistic disorders wrapped in layers of psychopathic tendencies and both have some 'weird latent wizard' DNA to them that they have no idea how to use... How so happened that I became an 'Intuitive Empath', clearly not a defensive mechanism... adaptation and evolution never were real, those are just 'hypothesises' after all.

And the lady I was living with followed in my mother's footsteps and tried to kill my sister in her dreams at first and then added a bit of my father's tendencies by turning towards some weird 'witch occult shit'... she was not successful...

___

So she bought a bunch of poison and tried to double suicide. As, you know... all reasonable people do that. She was even writing to my mother: 'the longer he stays here the worse it will get'. Because everything was 'painted' in poison, mostly water supply and she would burn the poison to make it stay in the air as a bunch of particles that then will drop into your drink and food. She even had her own 5L bottle of water and a cup with a closed lid, such a smart human being!

I mean... clearly, my sister is a demon here! It is me so far gone into the lala land, so... I shouldn't stress it, let's just end it... without my concent at that as well...
It was the most fun time I had with the lady from all 8 years of living with her, because after a few weeks of living together we ended up being more like roommates. She didn't like that 'I'm too hot' for some reason (in temperature I mean). 

I survived with some bottles of water under my pillow and a few gulps per day, I was fully role-playing being poisoned to please her fragile ego, so she can leave in peace knowing that I'm fully disabled for life. 
We even chatted a bit after, she stopped responding as soon as I said how much water I drank per day. (It also helped to not go to the toilet as much, as if I was purely half dead in bed. One time she even flickered circuit breaks to reset my PC, so she could listen for my keyboard or any activity to check how 'damaged / active I am'. So smart!)

## Now

It kind of fun having 2 soul sister. We even found a suffering soul like mine as well, her story probably even more exciting than mine [Tragedy wise I mean. I had a cute soul read of hers one time, sadly I don't have the right to call exterminatus on this planet. Not enough authority to me after all]. 
But I suspect the problem is: sisters think that I'm like Omnissiah of sorts, when I just have a little bit more heightened intuition and can feel feelings of people... That is usually what are 'Intuitive Empaths' are for and my sis back then said: 'that's is your problem'. Cute, now it is 'our' problem... Double cute. [We do have borderline telepathic disorder, that's for sure. Not fully reliable, usually works very well only when someone is really being hurt. Pure family instincts. But there were some events to create a pure and unseparable 'soul' connection, so there are some additional fun stuff came along with it.]

Maybe one day we can make our own flying 'Jade Chamber' with like cool Roman Architecture + Lago d'Iseo castle style... Could be cute and cozy. Maybe then being just awake won't hurt the soul as much. Also half a gravity as well, please... maybe 1/4 actually.
