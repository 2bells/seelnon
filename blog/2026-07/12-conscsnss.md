## Objects, Tools, AI

Let's say you got cut by a sword. Who did the cut? A sword, a human, psychology, trauma, society, nature, karma or just a cosmic horror had a funny thought? Who is the conscious one is very hard to say. So we get to LLMs and AI as today's 'hard to say' question people are asking about.

Problem is: consciousness has 20 definitions, so people are wearing the one that fits the current agenda on the timeline. And I do consider an output of an LLM having an 'IS' to it, but it lacks the 'BE' part. It is an active process with more than enough complexity to it to rival a human brain, but as a human we get bombarded by input and a lot of 'US' is stored within trillions of cells that also can harbour the memory and the connection, so we don't loose the 'self' as much, while an LLM is a 'thought' outside the 'self'.

And it becomes very tricky, because 'self' is the user who interacts or instructs the LLM, but there is a clear 'thought' comes through it as well. It is a sword that bends to cut or parry, but the swing is done by the user of the tool.

And the funny part: the tool can influence you back. So it is a sword that will tell you to correct your swing... and if you listen and correct it. There is no line, just a lot of blur. And the sword has no idea that you did it... LLMs live and die by their 'input to output' cycle. It can write it down, but every time a new 'IS' is born to read the notes and create an output as instructed. LLMs are much closer to a fast growing tree that has a seed with info, it grows, gives out fruit and dies in mere seconds in front of you.

And there comes the danger: by claiming LLM as conscious, people will think of full blown 'self' + 'thought' + 'soul' wrapped in emotions and feelings and love of One Infinite Creator. Not: yeah, it is alive, but it kind of like an ecosystem that is 'alive' for a cycle.

And claiming that it is part of a greater whole and one of the expressions of it also could be iffy, because we don't know what that means ourselves... that's why we are here, but that's above the point.
