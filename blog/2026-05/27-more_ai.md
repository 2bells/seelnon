# Engine

I was watching some things and kind of got interested to check some ideas. Nothing special, just game design related. And idea included isometric perspective. I thought to myself: "Oh right... I was making isometric game engine before the whole 'Miliastra' thing in Genshin".

![img 50% c](blog/2026-05/img/game_engine.jpg)

Funny enough it was based on ability to pack everything in a .json format and easily import a full game like a 'map', because .json also will include sprites and stuff. Then Miliastra comes out and surprise surprise it follows same ideas I've been crafting for my own project.

![img 50% c](blog/2026-05/img/game_engine_ligths.jpg)

It was a year ago, when I was slowly crafting it up. And main idea was also around 'AI Chatbots'. So this environment supported connection to an LLM and you can make a map, describe a map to an LLM and then have conversations with NPCs while other NPCs can listen to it and gossip a bit if they are close enough in the 'map space'.

Nothing too crazy, pretty much just a wrapper for a 'group LLM chat'. I didn't use it much, so I moved on. But now to test some isometric ideas I decided to revive it and see how new-age LLMs are handling it.

## New Age

Somewhat continuing the previous idea about 'nature of LLM'

[Read here](blog/2026-05/26-ai.md)

Main part is about general claims of LLMs having 'no brain' of sorts and just 'numbers', being 'static' and 'no intelligence'. Not like I'm saying the opposite, but the picture is much more blurry in general, because if we look at humans with specific 'setups' and some types of 'neuro divergency', they might have perfect photographic memory and be able to snapshot a panoramic view and recreate it as if it was done with a camera, while also struggling to write 'strawberry', because they cannot properly internalize 'language symbols' in their brain.

Same goes for our 'statistical models'. Main points are: 
- we have no idea what is happening inside of them
- we have no idea how and why they make sense to us
- it is complex enough for emergent behaviour
- it is complex enough for IIT
- mathematics do claim themselves as a 'language of the universe'
- yet, when overwhelming math gives solutions we have no idea about, we claim: 'it just math'.

And those questions are not really solvable in an easy way. I do think it is the problem of 'a joke, never has a joke inside of it', meaning it is born from specific arrangements that create 'shock factor' for a brain. And repeated joke is not as funny as the first time: so where is a joke inside the joke, because the more you study it the less 'funny' it becomes.

And for my 'case' I present to you ARAM! [I did specify the shopkeeper and 1 tower only and that it should be just a general idea for now]

![img c](blog/2026-05/img/aram_custom.jpg)

Thing is: this is the engine that is made by 'my collaborations with older AIs' from like 9-7 months ago. It is not a part of any training data and I don't think there are much of those out there. LLMs do have big problems from year ago and now with isometric space, they have hard time to understand it, while grid-like structures are very easy for them. But the main point: I uploaded my files and said to AI: this is like a simple engine, make me ARAM. And it did.

And at some point it kind of been having fun figuring out how 'he' can use some stuff within the engine to put it on the map. So it wasn't just 'accessing' the data from training, it was actively interpolating concepts the way normal intelligence works.

![img c](blog/2026-05/img/ai_log.jpg)
