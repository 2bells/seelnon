## History

When I was developing it back in the day, it was like a big 'Magnum Opus' project of previous generations of LLMs. I approached it with a lot of initial planning and design to make it very comfortable for models 2 generations prior to handle with comfort (back then it was Gemini 2.5, now Gemini 3.1 -> Gemini 3.5) and it seems like 3.5 has 0 problems of actually just 'doing the thing'. To the point that even refactoring is not that much of a problem anymore. I haven't tried new 'Claude Opus' and Claude usually always was a frontier of LLM code, while GPT would be the 'survey says: we are the best!' while being a complete meme in production... (not to be a conspiracy guy, but they probably asked it to do 'sparkly animated stuff' so when they run tests, users will pick GPT versions, because they look more 'stylish')

Claude also went through many annoying updates since 3.5... 3.7 was like a super active 'overachiever' who would just nuke everything, because it acts like "I HAVE THE VISION!!!"... And from 4.1+ forward I only was hearing about their prices being too much. And using 4.0 / 4.1 was weird as if half of it knows what is happening, other don't. (you can even figure it out by how fast they pushed +0.1 into the wilds)

But with agentic layering it seems that Gemini just 'works' nowadays. They don't really advertise, but every update feels actually like an update. Gemini 3.5 is the first model to actually complete a few refactors and don't break anything. But it still struggles with a lot of 'stylings'.

Overall, LLM code seems to do nicely and the human aspect  becomes the main problem more and more. Something I'm noticing that: the more you use it successfully, the more that feeling of the 'magic button' starts to creep in into your brain, but then, after everything goes wrong, because, well... it is still a blind painter painting you a picture... you, as a driver of the thing, suddenly realize, that autopilot is broken and you have to land the plane on your own... and it gives you a fun reality check: 'hey... you are on your own, always has been, good luck... I hope you paid attention during last 20 commits and know, what went wrong, and your backups are still there.'... Goddess of Failure blows you a kiss and now it is time to figure the spaghetti out.
